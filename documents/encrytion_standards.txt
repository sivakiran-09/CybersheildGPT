Data Encryption Standards

All PHI at rest must be encrypted using AES-256 or an equivalent industry-standard algorithm.
This applies to database storage, backup media, and any exported files, including EHR export
jobs and archived data bundles.

Data in transit must be protected using TLS 1.2 or higher for all connections carrying PHI,
including connections between internal systems and any third-party integrations. Legacy
protocols such as SSL 3.0 and TLS 1.0 must be disabled across all systems.

Encryption key management should follow separation of duties, ensuring that personnel with
access to encrypted data do not also control the encryption keys unilaterally. Keys should be
rotated on a defined schedule and immediately upon suspected compromise.

Removable media, including USB drives used for data transfer, must use full-disk encryption,
and their use should be logged and restricted to approved personnel only.
