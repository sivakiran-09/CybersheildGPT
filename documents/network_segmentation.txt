Network Segmentation Guide for Healthcare Environments

Network segmentation limits the blast radius of a compromised account or device by restricting
lateral movement between network zones. Clinical devices, such as infusion pumps and imaging
systems, should reside on a separate VLAN from general administrative workstations.

The EHR database tier should not be directly reachable from end-user workstations; all access
should route through an application layer that enforces authentication and logging. Firewall
rules between segments should follow a default-deny posture, explicitly allowing only required
ports and protocols.

Administrative access to critical systems, including domain controllers and EHR servers, should
be restricted to a dedicated jump host with multi-factor authentication and full session
recording. This significantly limits an attacker's ability to pivot from a single compromised
account to full domain access.

Segmentation should be validated regularly through penetration testing focused specifically on
lateral movement paths, not just perimeter defenses.
