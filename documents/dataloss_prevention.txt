Data Loss Prevention (DLP) Policy

This policy governs the monitoring and control of sensitive data leaving the organization's
network, including protected health information (PHI), payment data, and internal credentials.

DLP tools should be configured to flag or block large outbound data transfers, particularly to
unrecognized or newly observed external IP addresses. Email attachments containing patterns
matching medical record numbers, Social Security numbers, or insurance identifiers should be
automatically quarantined for review.

Exceptions to automatic blocking require documented business justification and approval from
the data owner. All DLP alerts must be reviewed within one business day, and alerts involving
PHI must be escalated to the privacy officer regardless of whether the transfer appears
legitimate.

Regular tuning of DLP rules is required to reduce false positives while maintaining sensitivity
to genuine exfiltration patterns, such as archive creation immediately preceding an outbound
transfer.
