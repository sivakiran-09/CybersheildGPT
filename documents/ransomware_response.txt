Ransomware Response Playbook

Ransomware attacks against healthcare organizations often begin with a phishing email or a
compromised remote access credential. Once inside, attackers typically move laterally through
the network before encrypting files and demanding payment.

Immediate containment steps include isolating affected systems from the network, disabling
shared drives, and preserving volatile memory and logs for forensic analysis before powering
down infected machines. Do not pay the ransom without legal and law enforcement consultation,
as payment does not guarantee data recovery and may violate sanctions regulations.

Recovery should prioritize restoring from known-clean backups rather than decrypting files with
attacker-provided tools. Organizations should maintain offline, immutable backups specifically
to survive ransomware that targets connected backup systems.

Post-incident, conduct a full root-cause analysis to identify the initial access vector and
close that gap before reconnecting systems to the network.
