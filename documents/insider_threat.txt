Insider Threat Program

Not all data breaches originate from external attackers. Insider threats include malicious
employees exfiltrating data for personal gain, as well as negligent staff who mishandle PHI
without malicious intent.

Behavioral indicators of a potential insider threat include accessing records far outside an
employee's normal role or patient assignment, downloading unusually large volumes of data, or
accessing systems at atypical hours without a documented business reason.

User and Entity Behavior Analytics (UEBA) tools can establish a baseline of normal behavior per
role and flag statistically significant deviations, such as an admin account suddenly querying
the entire patient database when its typical usage is under fifty records per day.

Distinguishing a compromised account from a genuine insider threat requires correlating the
anomalous activity with authentication context, such as login location, device fingerprint, and
whether the activity followed a suspicious authentication event like a brute-force attempt.
