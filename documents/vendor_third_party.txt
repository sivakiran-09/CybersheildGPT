Third-Party and Vendor Risk Management

Healthcare organizations routinely share PHI with business associates such as billing
companies, cloud hosting providers, and medical device vendors. Each such relationship requires
a signed Business Associate Agreement (BAA) under HIPAA before any PHI is shared.

Vendor risk assessments should evaluate the third party's security posture, including their own
incident history, encryption practices, and access controls. High-risk vendors, such as those
with direct network connectivity into clinical systems, should undergo annual security reviews.

Any vendor-caused breach of PHI is still the covered entity's regulatory responsibility to
report under the Breach Notification Rule, making vendor due diligence a direct risk-reduction
measure, not just a compliance formality.

Vendor remote-access accounts should be time-limited, individually attributable (not shared
credentials), and subject to the same MFA and logging requirements as internal accounts.
