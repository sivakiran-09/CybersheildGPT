Backup and Disaster Recovery Policy

Critical systems, including the EHR platform, must maintain daily backups with a minimum
retention of 30 days. At least one backup copy must be stored offline or in an immutable format
to protect against ransomware that specifically targets connected backup infrastructure.

Recovery Time Objective (RTO) for the EHR system should not exceed four hours, given the
patient-safety impact of prolonged clinical system downtime. Recovery Point Objective (RPO)
should not exceed one hour of data loss for critical clinical systems.

Backup integrity should be tested through regular restoration drills, not just verified via
successful backup job completion logs, since a backup that cannot be restored provides no real
protection.

Disaster recovery plans should account for scenarios beyond technical failure, including
extended ransomware recovery timelines, and should define clear decision authority for
declaring a disaster and initiating failover procedures.
